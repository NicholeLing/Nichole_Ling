THE THREAD / 共同的线索

Different settings.
One shared question.

What helps someone move from “I don’t know how” to “I can figure this out”? I’m drawn to that question in classrooms, on trails, and in research. My work begins by listening closely, then finding practical ways to make the next step clearer.

EDUCATION & TECHNOLOGY

Helping debaters ask better questions

As a debate coach, I saw how recurring beginner questions could take time away from deeper thinking. I created an AI chatbot to help students navigate foundational Public Forum questions and focus on reasoning and evidence.

Explore the debate archive ↗
CoachingLearning designAI
Read the learning insight

Students need both clear starting points and room to think independently. My aim is to make basic support easier to access so that coaching can focus on connecting claims, evidence, and reasoning.

OUTDOOR EDUCATION

Learning on the wall & the trail

At UCI’s ARC, I’ve helped beginners learn to climb and led outdoor trips. Listening to participants’ recurring questions inspired me to organize climbing techniques and knots into approachable learning resources.

RESEARCH & OUTREACH

Making evidence useful

My work spans research on electric vehicle demand and airline markets, alongside science and statistics outreach for high school students. Across both, I’m interested in turning complex information into something people can use.

EconomicsStatisticsScience outreach
Read the learning insight

Research asks us to examine evidence carefully; teaching asks us to make that evidence understandable. I value both the rigor behind an idea and the way it is communicated.
